title: Discord
description: Linking your Discord account to unlock your club's private chat, team announcements, and server roles.
---

Fubball runs an official [Discord server](https://discord.gg/S3S76wwy2) for matchday chatter, league notices, and [A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/) campaigning.

Connect Discord under **[My Account](https://fubball.online/account) → Connected Accounts** (or by signing in with Discord). Once linked:

- **Club role:** You automatically receive the Discord role for your chosen [club](/guides/choosing-a-team/).
- **Team chat & announcements:** Linking opens your club's dedicated team chat channel and team announcements feed, where match updates and club news are posted.
- **Transfers:** If you switch clubs on the site, your Discord role and club channels update automatically to match.

Connecting Discord also syncs [Achievement](/guides/achievements/) milestone roles as you collect honours.
