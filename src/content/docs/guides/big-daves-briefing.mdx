title: Big Dave's Unofficial Briefing
description: Your personal catch up sheet for everything that happened to your Pints, club, and bets while you were away.
---

If you miss a few matchdays, [Big Dave's Unofficial Briefing](https://fubball.online/rundown) gives you a quick catch up on what happened while you were away. Open it from the user menu. The indicator lights up when new updates have landed since you last checked in.

By default, Big Dave rounds up the Last 24 Hours, with Older and Newer buttons to step through earlier spells:

- **Pints Earned:** Total [Pints](/guides/pints/) pocketed and a breakdown of your payouts.
- **Your Club:** Recent match results for your [club](/guides/choosing-a-team/) alongside clubhouse updates and [A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/) outcomes.
- **Bet Results:** Settled and cashed out [bet slips](/guides/betting/) from that window, along with your net return.
