Fubball is free to play and independently run. Voluntary donations from the Support Fubball modal or [My Account](https://fubball.online/account) help keep the servers ticking, the floodlights on, and the half time pies warm.

<Infobox variant="fa" title="No Bribes">
Supporting Fubball buys zero competitive advantage on the pitch. It never grants [Pints](/guides/pints/), better odds, or extra votes.
</Infobox>

## Supporter perks

Becoming a League Supporter unlocks:

- **Exclusive Themes & Club Colours:** Access to 10+ site themes plus a custom theme styled in your favourite club's colours, along with ad free browsing where ads are enabled.
- **Supporter Badge:** Displays the ★ Supporter badge on your fan profile, your club's fans list, and [The Chalkboard](/guides/chalkboard/).
- **Submit a Player Name:** An optional chance during checkout to submit a first and last name into the F.A. rookie pool for review. Picks are not guaranteed, and you will be emailed if yours enters the league.
- **Discord Supporter Role:** Unlocks the private supporter lounge, behind the scenes updates, and direct chat on [Discord](/guides/discord/) once your account is linked.
