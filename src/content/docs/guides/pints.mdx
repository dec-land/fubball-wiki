<Infobox variant="fa" title="Virtual Currency">
Pints have zero real monetary value and cannot be bought with real money. Every new account starts with 250 Pints.
</Infobox>

Pints are the currency used across Fubball. You earn them by showing up and putting them back into the league:

- **Match wins:** Whenever your chosen [club](/guides/choosing-a-team/) wins a fixture, you earn Pints.
- **Betting:** Winning match bets and outrights on [My Bets](https://fubball.online/account/bets) is the main way to grow your balance. See the [Betting](/guides/betting/) guide for details.
- **Inviting a mate:** Share your link from **Share Fubball** in the account menu to earn bonus Pints when a mate signs up and places their first bet.

You can spend Pints in a few places:

- **Betting:** Stake Pints on live and upcoming fixtures.
- **The Bar:** Buy items behind the counter at [The Bar](/guides/the-bar/).
- **The A.G.M.:** Cast votes on league Directives and Club Demands every Sunday at [The A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/).
- **Club transfers:** Once your two free seasonal transfers are used, [switching clubs](/guides/choosing-a-team/) costs a Turncoat Tax of 100 Pints.
