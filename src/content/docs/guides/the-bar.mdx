[The Bar](https://fubball.online/bar) is where supporters spend [Pints](/guides/pints/) behind the counter on Bar Snacks, Beverages, and items Off The Menu.

What most items actually do is not written on the menu at first. Only once enough supporters have ordered an item does word get round behind the bar about what it is for.

Then there is the Pickled Egg, bobbing silently in a jar of cloudy brine on the counter. Nobody knows what it does, yet people keep buying one.

Below the main menu sits The Cellar, locked from the inside. Whatever is stored down there stays out of reach for now.
