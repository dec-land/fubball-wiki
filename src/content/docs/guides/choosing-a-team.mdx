When you join Fubball, you pick a club to follow from [Choose Your Club](https://fubball.online/choose-team). Pick for the badge, the town, the play style, or a terrace that needs more voices.

## Club play styles

Every club plays with one of six styles: Route-One Grinders, Prawn Sandwich Purists, Chaos Merchants, Canteen Union, Dark Arts Specialists, or Low-Block Bureaucrats. Each style shapes how a squad behaves on the pitch. Inspect any club on [Choose Your Club](https://fubball.online/choose-team) to compare them, or watch a few fixtures to see how they play out.

## Finding a club

You can search by club name, town, or shorthand, filter by play style, or filter by Needs Fans to spot under supported terraces.

If you are undecided, use **Help Me Pick** to take the F.A. Allocation Bureau's short sorting quiz for tailored recommendations, or pick an under supported club to join one of the smallest terraces.

## Switching clubs

You receive two free club transfers per season. Once those are used, switching clubs costs a Turncoat Tax of 100 [Pints](/guides/pints/).
