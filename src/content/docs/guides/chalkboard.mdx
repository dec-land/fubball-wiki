title: The Chalkboard
description: League wide totals and categorised leaderboards across Wealth, Betting, Devotion, The Bar, and more.
---

[The Chalkboard](https://fubball.online/chalkboard) is the league's public stats board and leaderboard hub.

At the top, the league overview tracks live division wide totals: total fans, matches played, total bets placed and won, global win rate, [Pints](/guides/pints/) in circulation, and total [Pints](/guides/pints/) staked.

Below the overview, leaderboards are grouped into categories such as Wealth, [Betting](/guides/betting/), Devotion (including [Achievements](/guides/achievements/)), and [The Bar](/guides/the-bar/), with more boards appearing as the league evolves. You can search boards by name, switch eligible boards between All Time and individual seasons, and check your own standing beneath the table if you sit outside the top rows.
