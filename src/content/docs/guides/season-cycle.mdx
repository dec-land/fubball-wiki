---
title: Weekly Season Cycle
description: How time works in Fubball, from Monday's opening kickoff to Saturday's playoffs and Sunday's A.G.M. (Annual General Meeting).
---

In Fubball, 1 in-game day equals 1 real-world hour, and 1 full season equals 1 real-world week.

The regular season lasts 95 matchdays (five complete round-robin cycles of 19 days each), with a 12-hour Midweek Break in the middle so fans can catch their breath.

## Weekly schedule (UTC)

| Day & Time (UTC) | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday 09:00** | Regular Season | Day 1 kicks off. Fixtures run every hour. |
| **Wednesday 18:00** | Midweek Break | Day 57 wraps up; the league pauses for 12 hours. |
| **Thursday 06:00** | Regular Season | Day 58 resumes the run-in. |
| **Friday 20:00** | Regular Season Ends | Day 95 ends and final standings are set. |
| **Saturday 09:00** | Postseason Playoffs | Best-of-3 playoff series determine the season's champion. |
| **Sunday 00:00 – 18:00** | The A.G.M. | Voting opens for the A.G.M. on league Directives and Club Demands. |
| **Sunday 19:00** | Offseason | A.G.M. results are applied, promotions and relegations take effect, and the league prepares for Monday. |

<Infobox variant="terrace" title="Midweek Break">
If no fixtures are running between Wednesday 18:00 UTC and Thursday 06:00 UTC, nowt is broken; the players are having their Midweek Break.
</Infobox>

## The A.G.M.

Every Sunday between 00:00 and 18:00 UTC, the league holds its A.G.M., where supporters cast votes on:

- **Directives:** League-wide changes that can alter rules, introduce new conditions, or reshape how matches behave.
- **Club Demands:** Targeted proposals affecting individual clubs and rosters.

Results are processed at 19:00 UTC, and the new season kicks off on Monday at 09:00 UTC.
