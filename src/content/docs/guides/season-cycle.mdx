---

In Fubball, 1 in game day equals 1 real hour, and 1 full season equals 1 real week.

The regular season lasts 95 matchdays (five complete round robin cycles of 19 days each), with a 12 hour Midweek Break in the middle so supporters can catch their breath.

## Weekly schedule (UTC)

| Day & Time (UTC) | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday 09:00** | [Regular Season](/guides/schedule/) | Day 1 kicks off. Fixtures run every hour (view the [live schedule](https://fubball.online/schedule)). |
| **Wednesday 18:00** | Midweek Break | Day 57 wraps up and the league pauses for 12 hours. |
| **Thursday 06:00** | Regular Season | Day 58 resumes the season. |
| **Friday 20:00** | Regular Season Ends | Day 95 ends and final [Standings](https://fubball.online/standings) are set. |
| **Saturday 09:00** | [Postseason Playoffs](/guides/playoffs/) | Best of 3 playoff series determine the season's champion. |
| **Sunday 00:00 to 18:00** | [The A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/) | Voting opens on league Directives and Club Demands. |
| **Sunday 19:00** | Offseason | A.G.M. results take effect and the league prepares for Monday. |

<Infobox variant="terrace" title="Midweek Break">
If no fixtures are running between Wednesday 18:00 UTC and Thursday 06:00 UTC, nowt is broken. The players are having their Midweek Break.
</Infobox>

For how Directives and Club Demands work on Sundays, see [The A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/).
