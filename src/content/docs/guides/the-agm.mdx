Every Sunday from 00:00 to 18:00 UTC, the league holds its [Annual General Meeting (A.G.M.)](https://fubball.online/election). Votes are tallied at 19:00 UTC, and whatever passes takes effect before the next season kicks off on Monday at 09:00 UTC. See the [Weekly Season Cycle](/guides/season-cycle/) for full timings.

<Infobox variant="fa" title="Voter Eligibility">
You need a verified email address on [My Account](https://fubball.online/account) to cast votes at the A.G.M.
</Infobox>

## F.A. Directives

Directives are league wide proposals that can rewrite the rules of fubball, introduce new systems, or change how matches behave for every club. You spend [Pints](/guides/pints/) to vote for the Directive you want, and only the top voted Directive passes each week.

## Club Demands

Demands are petitions targeting your own [club](/guides/choosing-a-team/), squad, or ground. Unlike Directives, more [Pints](/guides/pints/) put behind a Demand increase its chance of being granted rather than guaranteeing a single winner.

## Discussion and campaigning

Because Directives reshape the whole league and club supporters often need to rally behind the same Demand, most of the campaigning and debate happens in [Discord](/guides/discord/) before the 18:00 UTC cutoff.
