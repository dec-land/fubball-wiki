Pints cannot be bought with real money. Every Pint in circulation is earned through betting, showing up, and following the league.
</Infobox>

Fubball is a continuous browser-based fubball simulation played live, every hour, all week. You pick a club, watch fixtures unfold in real time, get your bets in, and vote on what happens to the league at Sunday's A.G.M. (Annual General Meeting).

The sport on the pitch is simple enough to follow on your first matchday. The strange bits (weather, referees, player vibes, and whatever else goes on behind the scenes) are for supporters to figure out together.

## The basics

- **Pick a club:** Choose a club to follow and cheer on across the season.
- **Watch live:** Fixtures kick off at the top of every hour. You can follow the live pitch feed or catch up on results later.
- **Bet with Pints:** Put Pints on upcoming fixtures to build your balance, then spend them in the Pub Shop or back your club.
- **Pick an Idol:** Follow a single player closely and keep an eye on their form and vibe.
- **Vote at the A.G.M.:** Every Sunday, supporters vote at the A.G.M. on league-wide Directives and club Demands, changing the rules and shape of the next season.
