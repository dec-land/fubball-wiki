<Infobox variant="fa" title="Official Notice">
[Pints](/guides/pints/) cannot be bought with real money. Every [Pint](/guides/pints/) in circulation is earned through betting, showing up, and following the league.
</Infobox>

Fubball is a continuous browser fubball simulation played live, every hour, all week. You pick a club, watch fixtures unfold in real time, get your bets in, and vote on what happens to the league at Sunday's [A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/) (Annual General Meeting).

The sport on the pitch is simple enough to follow on your first matchday. The strange bits (weather, referees, player morale, and whatever else unfolds) are for supporters to figure out together.

## The basics

- **Pick a club:** [Choose a club](/guides/choosing-a-team/) to follow and cheer on across the season.
- **Watch live:** Fixtures kick off at the top of every hour. Follow the [live pitch feed](https://fubball.online/live) or catch up on results on the [Schedule](/guides/schedule/).
- **Bet with Pints:** Put [Pints](/guides/pints/) on live or upcoming fixtures via [Betting](/guides/betting/), then spend them in [The Bar](/guides/the-bar/) or back your club.
- **Vote at the A.G.M.:** Every Sunday, supporters vote at [The A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/) on league wide Directives and Club Demands, changing the rules and shape of the next season.
