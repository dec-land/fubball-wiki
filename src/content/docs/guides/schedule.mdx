---
title: Schedule
description: How to read the 95 day fixture calendar, check live or upcoming matchdays, and open past Match Reports.
---

The [Schedule](https://fubball.online/schedule) page tracks every fixture across the 95 day regular season, where 1 in game day equals 1 real hour.

Select any day in the calendar to view that hour's slate of fixtures, filter matchdays by All, Finished, Live, or Scheduled, or use the Season Archive selector to check results from earlier seasons.

On any fixture card, you can [place a bet](/guides/betting/) on scheduled or live matches, jump to [Watch Live](https://fubball.online/live) while a match is in progress, or open a Match Report once it finishes to read the full event log.

For full weekly timings across the regular season, Midweek Break, and weekend, see the [Weekly Season Cycle](/guides/season-cycle/).
