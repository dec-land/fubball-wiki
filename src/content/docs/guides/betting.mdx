Betting is the main way to build your [Pint](/guides/pints/) balance for [The Bar](/guides/the-bar/) and [The A.G.M.](/guides/the-agm/).

## Placing a bet

You can bet on matches from a few places on the site:

- **Quick Bet:** On the [live match view](https://fubball.online/live), pick Home, Draw, or Away while a fixture is underway.
- **All Markets:** Open a match's full markets modal to bet on Match Winner, Total Goals (Over/Under 2.5), Both Teams to Score, or Total Cards (Over/Under 3.5).
- **Schedule:** Open upcoming fixtures on the [Schedule](/guides/schedule/) to get bets on before kickoff.

Odds update live as a match unfolds. Active match bets can be cashed out early from [My Bets](https://fubball.online/account/bets) while a fixture is scheduled or in progress and markets remain open.

## Limits and Seasonal Outrights

Match bets take between 10 and 250 [Pints](/guides/pints/).

On the Seasonal Outrights tab on [My Bets](https://fubball.online/account/bets), you can also place fixed stake bets on full season outcomes such as League Leader, [Championship Winner](/guides/playoffs/), and Bottom of the League. Outrights settle once the outcome is decided.
