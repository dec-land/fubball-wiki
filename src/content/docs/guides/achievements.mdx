[Achievements](https://fubball.online/account/achievements) are collectible terrace honours earned as you follow the league, back your [club](/guides/choosing-a-team/), place [bets](/guides/betting/), and witness whatever unfolds across a season.

<Infobox variant="fa" title="Zero Pint Reward">
Achievements do not pay out [Pints](/guides/pints/). They are strictly for pride, rarity tracking, and Discord milestone roles.
</Infobox>

Honours are split across five categories: Betting, Team, Player, Matchday, and General. Every achievement tracks global rarity, showing what percentage of fans have unlocked it, and linking your [Discord](/guides/discord/) account syncs milestone roles on the server.

Alongside the visible list, plenty of hidden achievements sit masked behind `???`. Some reveal themselves once you stumble onto the right trigger, while others stay out of sight until something changes in the league.
