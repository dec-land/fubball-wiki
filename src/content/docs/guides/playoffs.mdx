---
title: Playoffs
description: How the postseason Championship Cup works once the 95 day regular season finishes on Friday.
---

Once Day 95 wraps up on Friday at 20:00 UTC, the regular season [Standings](https://fubball.online/standings) lock and the postseason begins on Saturday at 09:00 UTC. See the [Weekly Season Cycle](/guides/season-cycle/) for full timings.

The top 8 clubs in the final table qualify for the Championship Cup knockout bracket, played across Round 1, Semifinals, and Finals. Every tie is a best of 3 series, where the first club to win two fixtures advances.

Anyone who backed the eventual champion on the Championship Winner outright market receives their [Pint](/guides/pints/) payout once the final series concludes. See [Betting](/guides/betting/) for how Seasonal Outrights work.

<Infobox variant="terrace" title="Subject to Change">
The postseason format is set by the F.A. and may change in future seasons. Check the [Playoffs](https://fubball.online/playoffs) page once Saturday rolls around.
</Infobox>
